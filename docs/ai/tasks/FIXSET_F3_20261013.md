## Findings and behavior
The available October12 latest.log has no wolf lifecycle/spawn records; debug.log's
wolf/Bogatyr matches are startup/mixin details. Neither proves an entity count or
the cause of the reported immediate roughly1000-wolf player incident. Current start,
view, admission and recovery paths do not automatically manufacture player wolves.
Legacy paid summon execution and archive recovery remain retired. No live world was
opened or purged, and no cap was added to legitimate player eggs/taming/breeding.

Source review found a concrete admission gap: identity conflicts were held only
after native insertion, leaving a copied conflicting wolf in the world. The join
guard now applies the existing identity decision before insertion; it preserves
the original directory/live wolf and existing review hold. Same native UUID/bond
reload, valid active archive destination and run retirement rules remain unchanged.
This fixes that gap; it does not establish it as the reported incident's cause.

Mercenary capacity is20 at all levels and counts saved unloaded identities. Legacy
rosters above20 retain their existing dogs; they cannot admit another until below20.
Base interval is600 ticks/30 active seconds. Retained approved progression is
ceil(600/(1+0.06*(level-1))), floor400 ticks/20 seconds, traced to
[the October5 approval](bogatyr-scaling-20261005.md).
One callback per native entity tick and at most10 elapsed ticks per decision prevent
repeated-event/offline catch-up. Failed placement retries after20 ticks, with no
successful summon/identity credit. Player and hired identities remain independent.

Patrol's native OwnerHurtTargetGoal now accepts successful eligible hostile attacks
from its master through the existing bounded threat history. History binds to
run/dimension/mode epoch. Stay and mode/owner transitions discard prior fights.
Allied pets/players, passive animals and unsuccessful hits do not gain outgoing
authorization. PR262 discussion_r4244287961 is implemented; no new targeting scan.
