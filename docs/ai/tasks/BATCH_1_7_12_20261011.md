## Implemented
- Feed Pack uses up to10 actual caller inventory items. Deterministic order: bones/rotten flesh, raw meat, other native wolf food; ties use player nutrition, item ID, then slot. Native food tags/components and authored stacks are preserved; ordinary bones gain no food properties.
- Loaded, living, currently owned run wolves only. Heal lowest-health wolves first, clipping to maximum; retain configured raw4/cooked8 health values. No debit when a heal is canceled or does nothing.
- Remaining inputs enable healthy adult pairs within8 blocks. Reserve the complete pair requirement before either feeding; already-loving mates need no extra item. No breeding debit for insufficient stock/no mate, pups, cooldowns, panicking/leashed/riding wolves, individually seated Patrol adults, or unsupported saved state.
- Feed Pack server actions revalidate class, live player/run, inventory guards, current stock and wolf eligibility. Captured generations prevent replay; same-tick requests are bounded. Client shows three accessible rows and truthful food availability.
- Native manual ally feeding and the skill share a temporary Stay mating permission. Original persisted anchors stay untouched; native BreedGoal owns mating/offspring/cooldowns. Follow cannot pull mating Stay wolves toward a moved owner.
- Birth, love/health loss, deadline or mode change ends the attempt, clears movement/threats and restores anchor return/sitting. Default timeout30seconds; bounded configured allowance max65seconds. Unload/reload cancels unfinished attempts. No invented pup, refund or cross-run preservation.
- Existing allied damage/negative-effect protections, native ownership/newborn enrollment and separate mercenary rules remain registered and unchanged.
