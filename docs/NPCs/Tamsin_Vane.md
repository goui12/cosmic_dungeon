# Tamsin Vane — Dungeon Entry Broker

Tamsin Vane is a living starting-area treasure hunter and dungeon entry broker.

## Player-facing role

- Found a tunnel route and a map to Base Camp marked with the initials **JHW**.
- Starts or frames the dungeon class-selector/group-queue flow where the build presents her at the entrance.
- Sold the route on one condition: survivors owe her a cut of treasure recovered with her map.

## Planned achievement: The Tamsin Tax

**Status:** planned/docs-only until a real player-facing Tamsin payment interface exists.

- **Title:** The Tamsin Tax
- **Design:** Awarded when an adventurer returns from the dungeon and gives Tamsin Vane a share of the treasure recovered using her map.
- **Flavor:** Tamsin calls it business. You may call it gratitude, obligation, or a poor decision made before seeing the haul.
- Locked behind completion of the first dungeon.
- Any item or currency can be used to pay this debt.
- Not repeatable.

No developer-only command should be used as the grant path. The achievement should become live only when players can pay Tamsin through a normal interaction.

## Lore

Tamsin is neither villain nor guide. She is the person who found the route first and had the nerve to put a price on it. If she seems too practical about the danger, remember that practical people are often the ones who survive long enough to bill you.
