# Naton Whitlock — General Supply Vendor

Naton Whitlock is a restored Village soul and the general supply vendor for dungeoneers returning from Dungeon 1.

## Player-facing role

- Sells practical basics: food, fire-making supplies, light sources, and containers.
- Serves as the first reminder that the restored Village is becoming a place dungeoneers can prepare, restock, and recover.
- Uses normal vendor transaction rules: locked access shows a reason, and completed purchases/sales update the player's currency balance server-side.

## Lore

Naton remembers ledgers, crates, and the comfort of ordinary preparation. He is not glamorous, but his shelves represent safety: a torch before the dark, a meal before the gate, and somewhere to put what survives the trip back.
