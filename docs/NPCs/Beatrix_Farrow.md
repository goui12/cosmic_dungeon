# Beatrix Farrow — Food, Campfire, and Homeward Memory

Beatrix Farrow is a restored Village soul tied to pig farming, food, Beatrix's Campfire, and Farrow's Chop.

## Player-facing role

- Provides the food-and-campfire side of the restored Village through the live D1 vendor profile `cosmicdungeon:d1/food_vendor`.
- Sells raw foods, Raw Farrow's Chop, Farrow's Chop, and Beatrix's Campfire.
- Beatrix's Campfire cooks Raw Farrow's Chop into Farrow's Chop.
- Farrow's Chop carries a faint interdimensional charge and returns travelers to the Main Village when used successfully.
- A successful Farrow's Chop return grants the live **Nostalgia Bait** advancement.

## Lore

Beatrix's kindness feels domestic until the dungeon reminds you how rare domestic things are. Her campfire smells like smoke, salt, and a home you may not have earned yet. Farrow's Chop is not merely food; prepared properly, it remembers the way back.

Her tone should remain gentle and bittersweet. She is not selling escape from the dungeon's story. She is preserving the feeling that escape is still possible.
