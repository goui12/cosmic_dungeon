# Eon Penrose — Apothecary of the Restored Village

Eon Penrose is a restored Village soul and the NPC face of the Dungeon 1 Brewing Store. See [docs/Vendors/Eon_Penrose.md](../Vendors/Eon_Penrose.md) for live prices and profile notes.

## Player-facing role

- Sells brewing ingredients, Glass Bottles, brewing equipment, standard potions, splash potions, and lingering potions to qualified players.
- General stock includes ingredients, Glass Bottles, Night Vision, and Fire Resistance.
- Theurgists can access Brewing Stands, Cauldrons, Healing II, Regeneration, and Lingering Healing.
- Theurgists and Judicators can both buy Healing and Splash Healing.

## Lore

Eon was an apothecary chasing a cure for a ritual sickness that no village physician could name. The deeper he studied the symptoms, the more the sickness seemed to study him back. Atlach-Nacha's influence turned careful remedy into obsession, drawing him below until his notes became a map of warning signs.

Restored, Eon still speaks like every potion is an argument with fate. He sells remedies because some wounds can be closed, and studies the rest because he refuses to let the dungeon have the final word.
