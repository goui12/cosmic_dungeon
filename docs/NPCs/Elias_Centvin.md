# Elias Centvin — Weapons, Tools, and Repair

Elias Centvin is documented in detail as a vendor at [docs/Vendors/Elias_Centvin.md](../Vendors/Elias_Centvin.md). This NPC note summarizes his story role without duplicating the vendor profile.

## Player-facing role

- Restored Village weapons and tools vendor.
- Supports repair-service/material themes for battered dungeon gear.
- Player-facing Dragoon lore frames repairs as salvage craft: a Dragoon values the weapon that returns, not only the weapon that strikes first.

## Lore

Elias treats every nicked blade and bent tool as a witness. His workbench is part armory, part confessional: dungeoneers bring him metal that survived the lower dark, and he sends it back with one more chance to matter.
