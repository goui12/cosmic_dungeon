# Dragoon Help Guide

Class ID: `dragoon`. Enabled for Dungeon 1.

A trident specialist and repair supporter. Land **100 valid melee or thrown trident hits** against hostile mobs to fill Chain Lightning. Hits must deal damage; sword hits, arrows, misses and immune targets do not charge it. At 100/100, press **Primary class skill (default G)** or its inventory button to arm your next valid trident strike. That strike releases the chain against eligible nearby enemies in line of sight. Dragoon mercenaries use the same 100-hit bar and automatically arm for their next valid trident strike.

There is one bar with no overflow bank. If the chain damages no additional enemy, it stays fully charged and armed for another strike. A useful chain spends the bar and earns one Chain Lightning proficiency success. Chain damage does not refill the bar or trigger another chain. Existing trident weapon progression still affects the triggering strike; Chain Lightning is a separate signature track.

Dragoons may use anvils. For **Repair Affinity**, invite a customer with `/repair <player>`; accept with `/repair accept <player>`, decline with `/repair deny <player>`, or cancel with `/repair cancel`. The customer supplies damaged supported gear and may offer an optional account-currency labor fee. The Dragoon supplies compatible repair components, including approved ordinary unmarked materials where accepted. The customer retains ownership. Both participants must remain nearby with the interface open and review the quote/readiness controls. Items, materials, and fees move only after successful validation; not every special item is supported. See [Repair Affinity](Dragoon_Repair_Affinity.md).

## Blackiron Sheath

Use **Class skill 1 (default Z)** or its inventory button to grant native Resistance I: 20% reduction of eligible incoming damage for 15 minutes. Includes the caster and same-party players, mercenaries and allied wolves within 10 blocks. Cooldown: five minutes. Dragoon mercenaries cast automatically when useful; equivalent Resistance potions do not add a second modifier.

## Shared rules and controls

See the [current player guide](../PLAYER_GUIDE.md) for party sharing, crafting/fuel/stacks, gear ownership, trading, progression and travel. [Controls](../PLAYER_GUIDE.md#controls-and-passive-progression) lists defaults and rebinding. The inventory class-name link opens this class in the H guide.

Hired mercenaries follow the shared [participation and healing rules](Signature_Skills_and_Controls.md#mercenary-participation-and-healing). At 30 unassisted kills from one spawner they warn, and at 40 refuse its enemies until a player walks and joins that fight or a party player dies. This includes owned wolves and secondary attacks.
