# Judicator Help Guide

Class ID: `judicator`. Enabled for Dungeon 1.

An oathbound mace-and-bow protector. Scintilla Vitalis and Lux Vitalis provide healing to living targets and harm undead; Ebonsight provides night vision, and Vielpiercer reveals targets with Glowing. Follow each authored item's tooltip and class restrictions. Mace/bow progression is passive. Eon sells the shared Theurgist/Judicator healing offers. Repair Affinity and anvil access belong to Dragoon.

## Smite

**Smite** charges from **10 credited undead kills**. Press **Primary class skill (default G)** or its inventory button to strike hostile undead around you. Judicator mercenaries activate Smite automatically when charged and a useful target is nearby. Allies, companions and non-undead are protected.

| Smite level | Skill | Radius | Damage | Successful activations to unlock |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Gravehammer | 3 blocks | 2.5 HP (1.25 hearts) | 0 |
| 2 | Bonegrinder | 4 blocks | 2.5 HP (1.25 hearts) | 10 |
| 3 | Deathpurge | 5 blocks | 5 HP (2.5 hearts) | 30 |
| 4 | Last Rites | 6 blocks | 5 HP (2.5 hearts) | 60 |

Each activation that damages at least one undead spends the ten-charge bar and earns **one** proficiency success, regardless of how many targets it hits. Kills fill charge; successful activations improve Smite. The bar stores no overflow, and Smite's own damage does not recharge it. If no mob takes damage, all charge is retained and the player sees: **Smite only works on undead! You did not damage any mobs**

## Strength buff

**Class skill 1 (default Z)** grants **Judicator’s Aegis**, +3 melee attack damage (1½ hearts) at Smite level 1. At Smite level 3, **Defender’s Bulwark** replaces it with +6 (3 hearts). Both last 30 minutes with a shared five-minute cooldown and include the caster and party players, mercenaries and allied wolves within 10 blocks.

Judicator mercenaries cast automatically when useful. Native Strength is shared with equivalent potions; ranks do not stack. Successful casts announce "<name> shares renewed strength for battle." followed by the skill name in blue italics.

## Shared rules and controls

See the [current player guide](../PLAYER_GUIDE.md) for party sharing, crafting/fuel/stacks, gear ownership, trading, progression and travel. [Controls](../PLAYER_GUIDE.md#controls-and-passive-progression) lists defaults and rebinding. The inventory class-name link opens this class in the H guide.

Hired mercenaries follow the shared [participation and healing rules](Signature_Skills_and_Controls.md#mercenary-participation-and-healing). At 30 unassisted kills from one spawner they warn, and at 40 refuse its enemies until a player walks and joins that fight or a party player dies. This includes owned wolves and secondary attacks.

During play, the primary meter sits above your hearts. Its bound key shimmers when ready.
