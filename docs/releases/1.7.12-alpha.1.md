# Cosmic Dungeon1.7.12 Alpha1

Bogatyr Feed Pack uses up to ten real inventory items, healing injured wolves before feeding eligible breeding pairs. Bones/rotten flesh come first, then raw meat, then other meat by player food value; ordinary bones remain unchanged.
The Wolfpack panel now has Patrol, Stay and Feed Pack. Stay wolves can temporarily move to breed after skill or ally feeding, then return to their original held positions after birth or a failed attempt. Old Kibble skills stay retired.
Includes server-side inventory/ownership/generation guards and native food, mating, offspring and reload regression coverage. Requires matching protocol30 clients/server. Licensed GUI/co-op/terrain acceptance remains TESTING.
Alpha release only; no TEST-server or local-client deployment.
