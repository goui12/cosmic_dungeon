# 1.5.10-alpha.1

Dragoon mercenaries now trigger chain lightning after 50 successful hits. Each Chain
Lightning level reduces the requirement by one hit, down to five. Lightning hits count
toward the next cast; casts are spaced at least one second apart.

A successful cast advances the existing Chain Lightning skill, with increasingly longer
level-ups. Levels appear in inventory group HUD hover and are announced to the group.
Charge and levels survive mercenary death and revival, then reset in the next dungeon.
Allies, pets and neutral animals are protected, and blocked casts earn no progress.

Alpha testing: verify chain targeting, hit credit, level-ups, reload and multiplayer
behavior on TEST. Player Dragoon mechanics and authored equipment are unchanged.
The matching loading-screen companion is published separately; CurseForge app delivery
of both components still requires licensed-profile verification.
