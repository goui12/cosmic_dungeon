# 1.7.2 Alpha 1
- Personal inventory now shares a class-themed layout with the original chest emblem and restrained lore artwork for the six enabled classes.
- The crafting grid, result slot, arrow, heading and recipe controls are gone. Hidden crafting slots cannot receive input; native item slots, armor, offhand, hotbar and Metalmancer's three extra slots keep their existing identities.
- Hover your class name for a blue underlined link; click it or activate it with keyboard focus to open your existing H class guide. Closing help returns to your inventory.
- Empty Skills space is mostly transparent, with a visible border and readable title, resource bar and entries. Saved movement/minimize/reset behavior is preserved.
- Creative item-picker tabs retain their native layout. Unclassified players get a neutral inventory; legacy inactive classes retain their emblem/color fallback without becoming selectable.
- Presentation-only change: no new network or save format, no class-chest content edits and no migration.

Testing: licensed GUI-scale/readability, drag/shift/hotbar/equipment movement and multiplayer acceptance remain TESTING. Potion crafting/resurrection controls and later class reworks arrive in their planned batches.
