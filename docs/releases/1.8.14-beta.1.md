# Cosmic Dungeon 1.8.14 Beta 1

- Charged Judicator Smite and Dragoon Chain Lightning can use G when Minecraft has no Quick Actions to open. Genuine conflicts identify the competing binding. A charged primary shows its bound key beside a meter just above the hearts. The key shimmers when ready.
- A Venefex mercenary tells its hirer when Banish Afflictions successfully cleanses the party. It waits for a harmful effect on a nearby ally. Its separate harmful potions require susceptible enemy targets.
- Each inventory party member with skill details has a disclosure arrow. The client remembers human choices by UUID and mercenary choices for the current run. Default collapse skills temporarily starts everyone closed. Switch it off to restore your saved player preferences.
- Group and Skills windows fit their content, with smaller skill rows and buttons and scrolling when space runs out.

To test, charge Smite in a solo Dungeon 1 run, press the displayed key near zombies, and watch for charge consumption and damage. With a Venefex hire, acquire a curable harmful effect and watch for its cleanse. Change GUI scale and health rows, toggle the global setting, and rejoin with a previously collapsed human member.
