# Cosmic Dungeon 1.6.12-alpha.1

- Add player Theurgist Skills crafting:20 Brewing Supplies for a random positive
  tierI splash potion, or40 for a valid tierII splash. No stand, bottle or fuel
  needed; a full inventory spends nothing.
- Prevent Theurgist positive thrown potions from affecting hostile mobs, including
  undead healing inversion. Preserve allies/pets/mercenaries and manual negative brewing.
- Offer resurrection to a dead teammate anywhere in the same active dungeon.
  Their death screen has Accept/Decline; successful acceptance costs the living
  caster120 supplies and restores the latest death position with five seconds of
  protection. No player cooldown or extra skill-level gate.
- Preserve separate mercenary resurrection rules, physical death drops and safe
  recovery of interrupted resource payments.

Testing Alpha: licensed visual, multiplayer and CurseForge app acceptance are pending.
Clients update through CurseForge; TEST receives the direct sFTP runtime update.
