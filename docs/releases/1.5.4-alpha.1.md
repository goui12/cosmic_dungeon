# Cosmic Dungeon 1.5.4-alpha.1

Batch 2: wolves and equipment drops.

- Player Bogatyr packs are uncapped for wolf eggs, taming, breeding, recall and recovery.
- Mercenary wolves remain separate run-bound summons with their own five-wolf limit.
- Active group members can equip mercenary wolves with wolf armor; ownership and ally protection remain intact.
- Mobs keep randomly generated difficulty armor, but that armor now defaults to no death drops.
  Server setting Loot.naturalArmorDropChance adjusts new generated armor only; authored spawner
  equipment/drop chances, held weapons and later picked-up/player-given equipment retain their rules.
- Existing wolf saves, world data and authored spawner presets remain compatible.
- Includes the matching optional client-only loading-screen helper.

Alpha testing is ongoing. Existing mobs retain their saved equipment drop chances.
