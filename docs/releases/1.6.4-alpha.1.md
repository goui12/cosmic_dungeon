# 1.6.4-alpha.1 - Batch 14 group health and effects

Every active dungeon human member, including yourself, and each hired companion has a health card with name/class, current/max HP, and vanilla effect icons below HP. Dead, offline and unavailable members never display old health as current. Positive and negative effects are included.

The world HUD fits all party members into columns as needed. A + marker indicates more effects; open inventory to see all synchronized effect icons. The inventory panel supports wheel scrolling and a visible click/drag scrollbar. Hover an icon for its effect and level, or a card for full names and companion skills/recovery details. With the recipe book open, the existing compact header retains health in its hover summary; close the book for full cards. The shared Skills/recipe-book redesign remains Batch 19.

Server snapshots reuse the existing bounded participant-only polling and send only changed values. Effect durations do not generate extra updates. Up to 64 effects per member are transmitted; extraordinary modded overflow is explicitly reported. Protocol 18 requires matching updated client and server jars.

No world-data/schema, authored spawner, inventory, equipment, or class-chest changes. No migration or datagen required. Manual licensed multiplayer/visual QA remains required.
