# Mercenary class roles
- Only Theurgist mercenaries brew and use potions. At a nearby accessible brewing stand, they instantly complete recipes from their own supplies without consuming player items or fuel in the stand.
- Other mercenary classes follow the leader and fight using their permitted equipment.
- Bogatyr mercenaries additionally summon one tamed dog every two minutes of active service, up to five per mercenary. Unloaded dogs still count; summons leave with the dungeon hire.
- Summoned dogs cannot attack allies, take allied damage or receive harmful potion effects from allies.
