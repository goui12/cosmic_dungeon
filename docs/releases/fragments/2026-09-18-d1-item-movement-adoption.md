# D1 protected movement and item authoring

Class-issued/no-drop items reject voluntary drops before removal. Protected equipment
cannot be inserted into shared or portable storage through ordinary menu clicks.
Owner inventory, ender storage, rename/enchant and reviewed repair slots remain usable.

Developer item adoption now previews the exact held or loaded-container stack before
explicit apply. Short-lived exact undo and an inventory survey support deliberate review.
No names infer identity, unopened loot tables generate nothing, and no world migration ran.

No required PNG or datagen. Death/clone, cursor overflow, automation and actual TEST menu/
mixin acceptance are separate tracked follow-ups. Not deployed.
