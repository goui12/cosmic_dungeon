# Cosmic Dungeon 1.6.0-beta

This beta completes the first mercenary update set and adds Tamsin introduction replay.
- Use the small top-right **i** beside Refresh to replay Tamsin's first-time agreement and map.
  Yes, No and Continue during replay affect only the preview; class, group, readiness,
  accepted onboarding and progression remain intact.
- Includes the alpha-tested mercenary identities, 50 Trace hiring/25 Trace revival,
  regeneration, per-run skills, material-free potion support, Bogatyr scaling,
  enemy-only Pyroclast fireworks and Dragoon chain lightning.
- Living Theurgist mercenaries offer resurrection at **Positive Potions level 10**:
  exact death location, five seconds of protection and a three-minute cooldown.
- Player Bogatyr packs remain uncapped and distinct from mercenary packs in this build.

Both the shared mod and client-only loading-screen jar are released with this exact version.
Use matching client/server mod jars (network protocol 16). No save migration or datagen is
required for the replay/version change. Existing authored worlds/chests/presets are preserved.
Companion CurseForge app delivery and licensed multiplayer/visual acceptance remain pending;
upload acceptance is separate from moderation. Stable promotion follows full beta testing.

The next round's trade/readiness, run-only wolfpack and player Skills panel changes are
planned separately; they are not included in this beta.
