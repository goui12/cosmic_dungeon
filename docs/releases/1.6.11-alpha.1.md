# Cosmic Dungeon 1.6.11-alpha.1

- Theurgists and Bogatyrs can request Brewing Supplies or Kibble from their dungeon
  group. Any living group member can donate eligible items after reviewing the request.
- Add a right inventory Requests column with independent cards, item/count previews,
  requester/class tooltips, Accept/Deny, bulk actions and wheel/slider scrolling.
- Consume only accepted eligible items and available resource capacity. Bulk requests
  process in visible order; changed inventory refreshes the preview without charging.
- Cancel old consent on death, logout or leaving the run. Preserve resource balances
  independently, and recover interrupted transfers without duplicate charges.
- Keep inventory screens open during recycling/accepting and explain group supplies
  in the H class guide.

Testing Alpha: licensed visual, multiplayer and CurseForge app acceptance are pending.
Clients update through CurseForge; TEST receives the direct sFTP runtime update.
