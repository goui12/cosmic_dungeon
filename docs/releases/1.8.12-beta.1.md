# 1.8.12-beta.1 — Shared class buffs

Unpublished source beta, Batch 4 of 6. Development source and GitHub only; distribution is held until the completed official Batch 6 build.

- Bogatyr Flurry of Fangs adds 10% melee attack speed for 30 minutes, including mercenary and wolf attacks.
- Dragoon Blackiron Sheath reduces eligible damage by 20% for 15 minutes.
- Judicator’s Aegis grants 3 extra melee damage for 30 minutes; Defender’s Bulwark replaces it with 6 at Smite level 3.
- Venefex Banish Afflictions removes harmful effects while preserving beneficial and neutral effects.
- All four use the remappable Class skill 1 key (default Z), a five-minute cooldown, and a 10-block party radius. Mercenaries cast automatically when useful.
- Inventory skill buttons show readable cooldowns. Native effect families prevent duplicate stacking, and ineffective casts spend no cooldown.
