# Batch 11 - trading during readiness and group labels

Trading while waiting for your leader keeps the group ready. Start Adventure safely cancels unfinished trades and returns offered items to their owners, including a trading partner outside the party. Full inventories keep refunds in the existing recovery queue. Completed trades remain completed.

New trades wait until dungeon entry finishes once the leader has submitted the adventure. The existing trade invitation expiry and adventure entry countdown are unchanged.

The group HUD hides readiness during an active dungeon. Mercenaries have no readiness labels, and their random name appears beside their class in the world, inventory and compact HUD.

Update client and server together: this revision uses network protocol 17. No saved-world migration or datagen is required. The matching client-only loading-screen helper is published separately.
