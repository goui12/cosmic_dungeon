# Cosmic Dungeon 1.7.10 Alpha 1

Batch10 adds the inventory potion palette for Theurgist and Venefex. Open Craft..., choose normal/strong/extended variants, and hover a native potion tile for effects and availability. Green means own materials, blue means consenting group materials, and red0 means unavailable.

Each click crafts one potion onto the cursor; matching crafted potions stack up to64. Shift-click crafts one into inventory, hotbar first. Complete vanilla ingredients and saved20-step brewing fuel are charged together. The fuel gauge is left of the recipes. Turtle Master remains drinkable-only for both classes; all potions remain tradable.

The Requests screen, random potion skills and live Brewing Supplies controls are retired. Legacy pending transfers still recover. Theurgist/Venefex Skills panels are hidden, with Resurrect... retained below Craft for Theurgists.

Server actions validate class, run, exact generation, current consent/inventories and output capacity. Native owner saves now preserve crafted cursor custody alongside inventory; old crafting schemas remain readable.

Alpha releases now finish after CurseForge publication. No Alpha sFTP/server checks or local-client JAR installation. Beta and official releases retain stopped TEST delivery. Protocol28 requires matching builds.
