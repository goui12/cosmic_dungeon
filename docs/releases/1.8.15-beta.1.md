# Cosmic Dungeon 1.8.15 Beta 1

- At Tamsin, choose **Yes** or **No** to share eligible crafting ingredients. Check **Always contribute** to choose Yes for this group and future groups. Uncheck it to be asked again.
- The final player's **Ready** puts the party into the entry queue, including a solo party. The existing instance countdown and trade refunds still apply.
- Forfeiting restores each party member to full health when they return. A fresh dungeon also starts at full health. Rejoining the same active run keeps current health.
- Dragoon Chain Lightning charges in **10** damaging melee or thrown trident hits against hostiles. Players press their bound Primary skill to arm the next valid strike. A mercenary arms automatically.
- Open the Help guide with **H** or through Skills. This release fixes the duplicate background blur that could crash the client on opening Help.
- Drag the **Skills** header to either side of the inventory. Available actions have teal borders, the selected action has a gold border, and hovering a button shows its description, state and binding when applicable.
- Spawner authors can use **Spawning → Mob scale** or look at a Cosmic Spawner and run `/spawner scale 0.5` for half-size mobs. The setting applies to future spawns, including their hitboxes. The default of 1 preserves authored sizes.
