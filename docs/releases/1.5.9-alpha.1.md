# 1.5.9-alpha.1 - Pyroclast fireworks

- Pyroclast player and mercenary rockets deal greatly increased damage to enemies while
  protecting players, friendly companions, NPCs and neutral animals.
- Pyroclast mercenaries prioritize rockets against nearby groups and replenish ammunition
  for free. Start with five; restore one every 30 active seconds.
- Fireworks levels speed replenishment and add a storage slot every two level-ups.
  Successful damaging bursts advance the skill and announce level-ups to the group.
- Rocket stock and progress survive mercenary death and reload, then reset for a new dungeon.
- Existing wall obstruction, terrain protection and bounded explosion processing remain.

Alpha gameplay testing is pending. Both mod JARs are published; companion approval and
licensed CurseForge profile update testing remain separate from upload acceptance.
